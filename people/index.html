Faculty, Students, and Alumni of the Recovering Sawhill Project

Faculty

Associate Professor of Engineering and Curator of Coins for the Madison Art Collection

Dr. Jason Forsyth is an Associate Professor of Engineering and the Curator of Coins for the Madison Art Collection at James Madison University. His major research interests are in wearable/ubiquitous computing and ancient numismatics. His current research interests focus on on-body human activity recognition and interactive machine learning for physical therapy patients and practitioners to increase exercise adherence and clinical evaluation.

Professor of Computer Science

Dr. Nathan Sprague is a Professor of Computer Science at James Madison Univeristy.

Director of the Madison Art Collection and Lisanby Museum

Virginia “Ginny” Soenksen is the Director of the Madison Art Collection and the Lisanby Museum at James Madison University. She holds an M.A. in Art History from Tufts University and a B.S. in Psychology from the University of Mary Washington.
